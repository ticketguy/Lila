\begin{abstract}
Local-first AI agents running on commodity laptops operate under tight memory, thermal, and startup-latency budgets without discrete GPUs. We present \textbf{ASI (Active System Image)}, a standalone C11/SIMD inference runtime and portable binary cartridge format (\texttt{.asi} v2) engineered for CPU-only execution and integrated into the Lila agent host via \texttt{AsiLlamaAdapter} and \texttt{LilaCore.\_boot\_gguf()}. ASI separates model distribution from execution using a 4\,KiB page-aligned, \texttt{mmap}-able cartridge (\texttt{AsiV2Header}, \texttt{AsiV2SectionEntry}, \texttt{AsiV2TensorEntry}) and executes hybrid state-space/attention architectures---specifically Qwen3.5 Gated DeltaNet + Grouped-Query Attention (\texttt{ASI\_ARCH\_HYBRID\_QWEN35})---with persistent scratch arenas, an SMT-aware spin-then-sleep thread pool (\texttt{AsiThreadPool}), integer-SIMD 2-row \texttt{dot2} and 1$\times$ row-unpack GEMM kernels (\texttt{Q4\_K} $\times$ \texttt{Q8\_K} and \texttt{Q6\_K} $\times$ \texttt{Q8\_K}), and crash-safe disk-persisted system-prefix snapshots (\texttt{ASIPFX02}).

In ASI v2.2 on \texttt{Intel(R) Core(TM) i5-5300U CPU @ 2.30GHz} (\texttt{Qwen3.5-0.8B} \texttt{Q4\_K\_M}, 2 threads), we integrate an automated \textbf{G0 correctness gate} (\texttt{correctness\_broadwell\_n64.json}), a hardware \textbf{roofline and active-weight audit} (\texttt{roofline\_broadwell.json}), and a statistical \textbf{G1 performance claim gate} (\texttt{claim\_verdict\_broadwell.json}). Under G0 across 320 positions (\texttt{N\_GEN=64} over 5 prompts), \texttt{ASI(Q8\_K)} achieves \textbf{320/320 (100.0\%)} batch-vs-token parity (\texttt{0.0} relative $L_2$), \textbf{316/320 (98.75\%)} teacher-forced top-1 agreement with \texttt{llama.cpp}, \textbf{315/320 (98.44\%)} agreement and lower mean logit relative $L_2$ error against unquantized \texttt{FP32-Ref} than \texttt{llama.cpp} (\texttt{0.047385} vs.\ \texttt{0.047615}, ratio $0.9952 \le 1.05$, all 4 flips at near-tie \texttt{FP32-Ref} margins $\le 0.0948$), and 5-subprocess determinism (\texttt{PASS} at 1 and 2 threads with \texttt{32/32} cross-thread agreement). Roofline measurements reconcile isolated 2\,MiB L3 kernel throughput ($\sim$27--42\,GFLOP/s) with full-model decode (518.34\,MB active weights streamed per token, 57.33\% \texttt{Q6\_K} including 40.24\% in tied \texttt{token\_embd}/\texttt{lm\_head}, against a 11.53/12.08\,GB/s DRAM read-only ceiling) and justify enabling \texttt{LILA\_ASI\_Q6K\_DOT2=1} and \texttt{LILA\_ASI\_Q4K\_DOT2=1} by default, lifting idle 2T $3\times 32$-token decode from 8.88 to 13.58\,tok/s. Under G1 (\texttt{claim\_check.py} over $N=15$ prepopulated pairs), ASI achieves tier \textbf{BEATS} with the verified claim \emph{``Beats llama.cpp on Intel(R) Core(TM) i5-5300U CPU @ 2.30GHz (2 threads)''}: \texttt{WIN} on Turn-1 TTFT (0.908 vs.\ 1.094\,s, $+17.1\%$, $p=0.00105$), Turn-2 TTFT (0.487 vs.\ 0.607\,s, $+19.9\%$, $p=0.003691$), and 939-token prefill (52.799 vs.\ 46.213\,tok/s, $+14.3\%$, $p=0.0003609$), statistical \texttt{PARITY} on Turn-1/Turn-2 decode, and non-overlapping \texttt{STRONG WIN} on RSS (616.266 vs.\ 978.223\,MiB, $-37.0\%$, $p=3.341\times 10^{-6}$), peak HWM (638.824 vs.\ 1406.730\,MiB, $-54.6\%$), and load time (0.053 vs.\ 2.182\,s, $-97.5\%$). Both the $N=15$ Broadwell capture and a companion $N=10$ Colab Xeon run (\texttt{4e73276}) predate v2.2 \texttt{dot2=1} defaults and SMT spin-backoff, and AVX-512/NEON kernels are compile- and audit-verified only.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction, Motivation, and Contributions}

Deploying an interactive local assistant such as Lila on an older, CPU-only laptop (2 physical cores / 4 logical CPUs, DDR3-1600, no GPU) exposes three coupled bottlenecks that server-oriented inference engines rarely optimize simultaneously:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Cold-start and memory-footprint overhead:} Parsing unstructured metadata, eagerly duplicating weight buffers, and allocating transient per-call scratch memory inflate resident set size (RSS) and startup latency.
    \item \textbf{Turn-to-turn system-prompt re-evaluation:} Agentic workloads prepend long system instructions and tool schemas (often 800+ tokens) on every turn; without persistent prefix state across restarts or sessions, scarce CPU cycles are wasted recomputing identical hybrid DeltaNet and KV caches.
    \item \textbf{Host-engine coupling and uncalibrated microbenchmarks:} Bundling tokenizer, tensor layouts, and orchestration inside a monolithic runtime complicates numerical auditing, while isolated L3-resident kernel benchmarks can severely mispredict DRAM-bound decode throughput without hardware roofline reconciliation.
\end{enumerate}

ASI v2.2 contributes four engineering advances:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Zero-Copy \texttt{.asi} v2 Cartridge and C11 ABI:} A 4\,KiB page-aligned format (\texttt{source/engine/asi/asi\_v2.h}) with 64-byte aligned weights (\texttt{MAP\_PRIVATE} / \texttt{MapViewOfFile}) and a minimal C ABI (\texttt{source/engine/asi/lila\_asi.h}) integrated into \texttt{source/src/core/asi\_backend.py} and \texttt{source/src/core/lilacore.py}.
    \item \textbf{Crash-Safe Disk Prefix Snapshot (\texttt{ASIPFX02}) and SMT-Aware Pool:} Atomic \texttt{.asip} prefix caching (\texttt{AsiPrefixSnapshotHeader}, dual CRC-64 ECMA checksums, \texttt{<filepath>.tmp.<pid>} rename, \texttt{0o700}/\texttt{0o600} permissions, LRU pruning) paired with topology-aware \texttt{AsiThreadPool} spin-backoff (\texttt{asi\_pool.h}) for SMT-oversubscribed VMs.
    \item \textbf{Roofline-Reconciled Kernels and Bit-Exact AVX-512 Alignment:} DRAM-vs-L3 characterization explaining full-model bottlenecks (57.33\% \texttt{Q6\_K} bytes, 40.24\% in tied \texttt{token\_embd}/\texttt{lm\_head}), default \texttt{dot2} \texttt{Q4\_K}/\texttt{Q6\_K} matvec execution in \texttt{asi\_arch.c}, branchless scale unpacking (\texttt{lila\_q4k\_unpack\_scales\_avx2}), and bit-exact 8-lane reduction across AVX2 and AVX-512 paths in \texttt{q4k.h}.
    \item \textbf{Automated G0 Correctness and G1 Claim Gates:} Disciplined verification via \texttt{test\_correctness\_gate.py}, \texttt{bench\_roofline.c}, and \texttt{claim\_check.py} separating verified Broadwell results from pre-v2.2 captures and compile-only ISA tiers.
\end{itemize}

\section{The \texttt{.asi} v2 Cartridge Format}

The \texttt{.asi} v2 specification is defined in \texttt{source/engine/asi/asi\_v2.h} and implemented in \texttt{asi\_v2.c}, \texttt{asi\_v2.py}, and \texttt{gguf\_to\_asi\_v2.py}. All on-disk structures are little-endian and enforced at compile time with \texttt{\_Static\_assert}.

\subsection{File Header and Section Directory}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Header (\texttt{AsiV2Header}, 64\,bytes):} Stores \texttt{magic = 0x41534921u} (\texttt{"ASI!"}), \texttt{version = 2u}, \texttt{arch\_id} (\texttt{AsiV2ArchId}: \texttt{1 = dense\_llama}, \texttt{2 = hybrid\_qwen35}, \texttt{3 = dense\_gemma}, \texttt{4 = dense\_qwen2}, \texttt{5 = dense\_qwen3}), capability \texttt{flags} (\texttt{ASI\_V2\_FLAG\_HAS\_VISION}, \texttt{HAS\_AUDIO}, \texttt{HAS\_ADAPTERS}, \texttt{HAS\_SIGNATURE}, \texttt{HAS\_CHAT\_TEMPLATE}), \texttt{n\_sections}, \texttt{total\_size}, \texttt{section\_table\_offset}, and a 24-byte \texttt{identity\_hash} (first 24 bytes of SHA-256 over section payloads in \texttt{write\_asi\_v2}).
    \item \textbf{Section Table (\texttt{AsiV2SectionEntry}, 32\,bytes/entry):} Each entry (\texttt{ASI\_SEC\_MODEL\_CONFIG = 0x01}, \texttt{ASI\_SEC\_WEIGHTS = 0x02}, \texttt{ASI\_SEC\_TOKENIZER = 0x04}, \texttt{ASI\_SEC\_METADATA = 0x08}, \texttt{ASI\_SEC\_CHAT\_TEMPLATE = 0x09}, \texttt{ASI\_SEC\_VISION = 0x0A}, etc.) has a 4\,KiB page-aligned \texttt{offset} (\texttt{ASI\_V2\_PAGE\_SIZE = 4096u}), \texttt{size}, and 64-bit \texttt{checksum}. Legacy sections (\texttt{0x05}, \texttt{0x06}) are ignored in the v2 load path; the Lila host retains sole ownership of tools and permissions.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Integrity Verification and Lazy \texttt{mmap}}
Checksums use \textbf{CRC-64 ECMA} (\texttt{0x42F0E1EBA9EA3693ULL} in \texttt{asi\_v2\_crc64}). During \texttt{asi\_v2\_validate\_memory}, small metadata, config, chat-template, and tokenizer sections are always CRC-64 verified on open, whereas bulk binary sections (\texttt{ASI\_SEC\_WEIGHTS}, \texttt{ASI\_SEC\_VISION}, \texttt{ASI\_SEC\_AUDIO}) skip eager CRC-64 scanning unless \texttt{LILA\_ASI\_VERIFY\_CRC=1} is set. Preserving lazy \texttt{mmap} avoids hundreds of megabytes of cold disk reads during \texttt{lila\_asi\_open}.

\subsection{Model Config and 64-Byte Aligned Weights Directory}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{\texttt{AsiV2ModelConfig} (96\,bytes):} Captures dense and hybrid SSM hyperparameters (\texttt{n\_layers}, \texttt{hidden\_size}, \texttt{intermediate\_size}, \texttt{n\_heads}, \texttt{n\_kv\_heads}, \texttt{head\_dim}, \texttt{vocab\_size}, \texttt{max\_seq\_len}, \texttt{rope\_dim}, \texttt{rope\_theta}, \texttt{rms\_norm\_eps}, \texttt{embed\_scale}, \texttt{full\_attention\_interval}, \texttt{ssm\_conv\_kernel}, \texttt{ssm\_state\_size}, \texttt{ssm\_group\_count}, \texttt{ssm\_inner\_size}, \texttt{ssm\_time\_step\_rank}).
    \item \textbf{\texttt{ASI\_SEC\_WEIGHTS}:} Opens with a 16-byte \texttt{AsiV2WeightsHeader} (\texttt{n\_tensors}, \texttt{flags}, \texttt{data\_offset}) followed by \texttt{n\_tensors} $\times$ 80-byte \texttt{AsiV2TensorEntry} descriptors. In \texttt{pack\_weights\_section}, \texttt{data\_offset} and every tensor payload size are rounded to 64-byte boundaries (\texttt{(n + 63) \& ~63}) so SIMD weight reads start cache-line aligned.
    \item \textbf{Optional AVX2 \texttt{Q6\_K} Repacking (\texttt{ASI\_TENSOR\_LAYOUT\_AVX2\_REPACK}):} \texttt{gguf\_to\_asi\_v2.py} supports \texttt{asi\_v2\_repack\_q6k} into 288-byte \texttt{LilaQ6KRepackedBlock} structs (\texttt{u6[256]}, \texttt{scales[16]}, FP32 \texttt{d}, \texttt{pad[12]}); all benchmarked artifacts here use standard unpacked GGML layouts (\texttt{repacked\_tensors = 0}).
\end{itemize}

\section{Runtime Architecture (v2.2)}

\subsection{Public C ABI and Host Integration}
\texttt{lila\_asi.h} and \texttt{lila\_asi.c} export model lifecycle (\texttt{lila\_asi\_open}, \texttt{lila\_asi\_close}, \texttt{lila\_asi\_model\_info}, \texttt{lila\_asi\_model\_header}, \texttt{lila\_asi\_get\_section}), session lifecycle (\texttt{lila\_asi\_session\_create}, \texttt{free}, \texttt{reset}), inference (\texttt{lila\_asi\_eval\_token}, \texttt{lila\_asi\_eval\_batch}, \texttt{lila\_asi\_sample}), and both memory-resident (\texttt{lila\_asi\_session\_save\_prefix} / \texttt{restore\_prefix}) and disk-backed (\texttt{lila\_asi\_session\_save\_prefix\_file} / \texttt{load\_prefix\_file}) prefix snapshots versioned by \texttt{LILA\_ASI\_NUMERICS\_VERSION} (\texttt{2U}). On the host side, \texttt{AsiLlamaAdapter} (\texttt{source/src/core/asi\_backend.py}) extracts zero-copy tokenizer and chat-template sections, manages prefix caching, and integrates into \texttt{LilaCore.\_boot\_gguf()} (\texttt{source/src/core/lilacore.py}).

\subsection{SMT-Aware Thread Pool (\texttt{asi\_pool.h})}
\texttt{AsiThreadPool} eliminates OpenMP barrier latency on tight per-layer dispatches and places hot synchronization atomics (\texttt{fn}, \texttt{gen}, \texttt{done\_count}, \texttt{active}, \texttt{sleeping}, \texttt{shutdown}) on distinct 64-byte cache lines (\texttt{\_Alignas(64)}). In v2.2, \texttt{asi\_detect\_physical\_cores()} parses \texttt{/sys/devices/system/cpu/cpu*/topology/thread\_siblings\_list} (with \texttt{sysconf(\_SC\_NPROCESSORS\_ONLN)/2} fallback) to adapt spin-backoff to SMT topology:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Dedicated physical cores (\texttt{n\_threads <= physical\_cores}):} Workers spin with \texttt{asi\_cpu\_pause()} up to \texttt{spin\_limit = 2048} iterations and \texttt{sched\_yield()} up to \texttt{yield\_limit = 4096} before sleeping on \texttt{pthread\_cond\_wait}.
    \item \textbf{SMT oversubscription (\texttt{n\_threads > physical\_cores}):} On 1-core / 2-SMT cloud VMs (e.g., Google Colab Xeon), spinning starves the hyperthread sibling; ASI drops automatically to \texttt{spin\_limit = 64} and \texttt{yield\_limit = 128}.
    \item \textbf{Manual override:} \texttt{LILA\_ASI\_SPIN\_LIMIT} overrides \texttt{spin\_limit} explicitly with \texttt{yield\_limit = 2 * spin\_limit}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Hybrid Qwen3.5 Execution and \texttt{AsiRuntimeConfig} (\texttt{asi\_arch.c})}
\texttt{asi\_arch.c} executes \texttt{g\_asi\_arch\_hybrid\_qwen35} across \texttt{QWEN35\_LAYER\_DELTANET} (1D causal convolution via AVX2 \texttt{\_MM\_TRANSPOSE4\_PS} + \texttt{lila\_expf\_avx2} in \texttt{asi\_conv1d\_worker} plus tiled recurrent DeltaNet updates) and \texttt{QWEN35\_LAYER\_FULL\_ATTN} (gated Q/K/V attention, Q/K RMSNorm, partial RoPE, grouped GQA KV reuse in \texttt{asi\_kv\_prep\_batch\_worker}/\texttt{asi\_full\_attn\_batch\_worker}, FP16 KV caches). During prefill, \texttt{qwen35\_forward\_batch} processes chunks up to \texttt{chunk\_max = 512} tokens using parallel \texttt{asi\_norm\_quant\_pool} and 64-byte aligned scratch (\texttt{ensure\_batch\_scratch}), while \texttt{q4k\_matmul\_batch\_q8k\_range} and \texttt{q6k\_matmul\_batch\_q8k\_range} unpack each weight row once per chunk into L1d and accumulate into a contiguous \texttt{tile\_out} transpose buffer.

During decode, \texttt{asi\_matvec\_range} now wires both \texttt{q4k\_dot2\_q8k\_avx2} and \texttt{q6k\_dot2\_q8k\_avx2} controlled by \texttt{AsiRuntimeConfig}:

\begin{lstlisting}[caption={ASI v2.2 runtime tuning, prefix cache, and audit environment variables.}]

\subsection{Crash-Safe Disk-Persisted Prefix Snapshot (\texttt{ASIPFX02})}
To persist evaluated system prompts across cold subprocess restarts:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Validated Binary Format:} \texttt{AsiPrefixSnapshotHeader} (144\,bytes, \texttt{\_Static\_assert}-checked in \texttt{asi\_arch.c}) carries magic \texttt{"ASIPFX02"}, \texttt{LILA\_ASI\_NUMERICS\_VERSION} (\texttt{2U}), model geometry/identity binding, and independent \textbf{CRC-64 ECMA} checksums \texttt{header\_crc64} and \texttt{payload\_crc64} protecting serialized DeltaNet and KV states.
    \item \textbf{Atomic Write Protocol:} \texttt{lila\_asi\_session\_save\_prefix\_file} writes \texttt{<filepath>.tmp.<pid>}, calls \texttt{fflush} and \texttt{fsync}, and atomically promotes via \texttt{rename}. \texttt{lila\_asi\_session\_load\_prefix\_file} validates magic, version, header CRC-64, and payload CRC-64 before mutating live session buffers.
    \item \textbf{Private LRU Host Cache:} \texttt{AsiLlamaAdapter.\_compute\_prefix\_key\_hash} hashes \texttt{b"ASIPFX02"}, cartridge \texttt{identity\_hash}, \texttt{arch\_id}, \texttt{fp32\_ref}, token count, UTF-8 system text, and integer token IDs into \texttt{prefix\_<hex16>.asip}. Cache directories are created with \texttt{0o700} (\texttt{secure\_private\_directory}) and snapshots with \texttt{0o600} (\texttt{restrictive\_umask()}), bounded by LRU eviction (\texttt{LILA\_ASI\_PREFIX\_CACHE\_MAX\_FILES=8}) and wired into \texttt{LilaCore.\_boot\_gguf()} at \texttt{Path(self.memory\_dir) / "prefix\_cache"}.
\end{itemize}

\section{Quantization and SIMD Kernel Design}

All quantized dot products and batched GEMMs reside in \texttt{source/engine/runtime/q4k.h}.

\subsection{Activation Quantization (\texttt{LilaQ8KBlock}) and 2-Row \texttt{dot2} Reuse}
FP32 activations are quantized dynamically into 256-element \texttt{LilaQ8KBlock} structs containing symmetric scale $d = \max_{0 \le i < 256}|x_i| / 127$, \texttt{int8\_t qs[256]}, and 16 sub-block sums \texttt{int16\_t bsums[16]}. In batched prefill, \texttt{quantize\_vec\_q8k\_strided} stores activations in block-major, token-strided order (\texttt{qx\_all + (size\_t)b * n\_tokens + tok}) so one weight block feeds 4 tokens simultaneously. In single-token decode, \texttt{q4k\_dot2\_q8k\_avx2} and \texttt{q6k\_dot2\_q8k\_avx2} keep \texttt{qx[b]} resident in registers across two adjacent weight rows (\texttt{row}, \texttt{row+1}), halving activation load traffic across DRAM-heavy \texttt{lm\_head}, \texttt{ffn}, and \texttt{dn\_proj} layers.

\subsection{AVX-512 Bit-Exact Reduction, Branchless Scales, and ISA Compile Audit}
In v2.2, \texttt{\#if defined(\_\_AVX512F\_\_)} kernels in \texttt{q4k.h} and \texttt{asi\_arch.c} were refactored to fold their 256-bit lanes into the bit-exact 8-lane \texttt{\_\_m256 sum\_vec} + \texttt{lila\_hsum\_f32\_avx2} reduction tree and shared \texttt{tile\_out} unpack (\texttt{lila\_q4k\_unpack\_row\_avx2}, \texttt{lila\_q6k\_unpack\_row\_avx2}) used by \texttt{\#if defined(\_\_AVX2\_\_)}. Additionally, branchy \texttt{get\_scale\_min\_k4} indexing inside \texttt{Q4\_K} inner loops was replaced by \texttt{lila\_q4k\_unpack\_scales\_avx2}. \section{Correctness and G0 Claim Gate}

Quantized integer-SIMD divergence must be audited end-to-end across all 24 layers because tight top-1/top-2 logit ties can branch greedy trajectories. ASI v2.2 combines unit checks with the automated \textbf{G0 gate} in \texttt{claim\_check.py}:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Unit and Snapshot Integrity (\texttt{test\_quant\_kernels.c}, \texttt{test\_asi\_v2.c}):} Validates \texttt{Q4\_K}/\texttt{Q6\_K} matvec relative $L_2 < 0.01$ vs.\ scalar FP32 dequantization, cartridge metadata and CRC-64 corruption rejection, and \texttt{ASIPFX02} disk prefix round-trip equality (\texttt{memcmp == 0}) plus tamper rejection.
    \item \textbf{Host Integration (\texttt{qualify\_asi\_backend.py}):} Executes 6 end-to-end checks on \texttt{AsiLlamaAdapter} and \texttt{LilaDaemon}: batch-vs-token parity, multi-turn tool calls, cancellation/session isolation, disk prefix cache persistence (\texttt{hit\_b='disk'}) and invalidation (\texttt{mut\_hit='miss'}) with 5-cycle RSS check, mTLS \texttt{/chat}, and controlled latencies.
    \item \textbf{Formal G0 Correctness Gate (\texttt{test\_correctness\_gate.py}, \texttt{claim\_check.py}):} Evaluates \texttt{Qwen3.5-0.8B} (\texttt{n\_ctx=512}, \texttt{n\_threads=2}, \texttt{N\_GEN=64} over 5 prompts = 320 positions) across \texttt{ASI(token)}, \texttt{ASI(batch)}, \texttt{llama.cpp}, and \texttt{ASI(FP32-Ref)} (\texttt{LILA\_ASI\_FP32\_REF=1}). Let $\mathrm{RelL2}(z, z_{\mathrm{ref}}) = \|z - z_{\mathrm{ref}}\|_2 / (\|z_{\mathrm{ref}}\|_2 + \epsilon)$ over the 248,320-token vocabulary logits; passing G0 requires:
\end{enumerate}

\begin{equation}
\label{eq:g0_gate}
\mathrm{Agree}_{\mathrm{batch,tok}} = 100\%,\quad
\mathrm{Agree}_{\mathrm{TF}} \ge 98.0\%,\quad
R_{\mathrm{FP32}} = \frac{\overline{\mathrm{RelL2}}(\mathrm{ASI}, \mathrm{FP32\text{-}Ref})}{\overline{\mathrm{RelL2}}(\mathrm{llama.cpp}, \mathrm{FP32\text{-}Ref})} \le 1.05,
\end{equation}
over $\ge 320$ positions, alongside $\ge 5$ independent subprocess determinism runs at \texttt{threads=1,2} with zero intra- or cross-thread token divergence.

\section{Experimental Methodology, Roofline Harness, and G1 Gate}

\subsection{Benchmark Hygiene (\texttt{bench\_strict\_bar.py})}
Both Broadwell and Colab evaluations share the exact GGUF (\texttt{Qwen3.5-0.8B-heretic-Q4\_K\_M-imatrix.gguf}, SHA-256 \texttt{d9662c91079317aee661a1475d61412787696e0dcc5636c39648fb27d49e1edf}) and \texttt{.asi} cartridge (SHA-256 \texttt{757731964e8a24950f3ae743be9df81ca5671f15b014aa45c31868131679e9f0}, 642,277,376\,B). Prompts (\texttt{T1\_PROMPT}: 53 tokens; \texttt{T2\_DELTA}: 27 tokens; \texttt{LONG\_PROMPT}: 939 tokens) are tokenized once into matching \texttt{int32} token-ID arrays, executed under matched parameters (\texttt{n\_ctx=2048}, \texttt{n\_threads=n\_threads\_batch=2}, \texttt{n\_batch=512}, \texttt{use\_mmap=True}, \texttt{flash\_attn=False}, greedy sampling, verified \texttt{t2\_eval\_tokens == 27}) in interleaved subprocess pairs after 1 discarded warmup per engine, with mandatory \texttt{-{}-prepopulate} (\texttt{madvise(MADV\_WILLNEED)} + 4\,KiB page touch).

\subsection{Hardware Roofline Harness (\texttt{bench\_roofline.c})}
\texttt{bench\_roofline} measures 256\,MiB AVX2 sequential read-only bandwidth ($BW_{\mathrm{DRAM\text{-}RO}}$), 80\,MiB STREAM Copy/Triad, isolated L3 (2\,MiB) and DRAM (64\,MiB) \texttt{Q4\_K}/\texttt{Q6\_K} \texttt{dot1}/\texttt{dot2} throughput, exact active decode bytes $W_{\mathrm{active}}$, and 32-token full-model decode against the DRAM-bound roofline:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:roofline}
T_{\mathrm{dram}} = \frac{W_{\mathrm{active}}}{BW_{\mathrm{DRAM\text{-}RO}}},\qquad
\mathrm{TPS}_{\mathrm{roof}} = \frac{1}{T_{\mathrm{dram}}},\qquad
U_{\mathrm{RO}} = \frac{W_{\mathrm{active}} \cdot \mathrm{TPS}_{\mathrm{meas}}}{BW_{\mathrm{DRAM\text{-}RO}}} \times 100\%.
\end{equation}

\subsection{Formal G1 Statistical Claim Gate (\texttt{claim\_check.py})}
To distinguish validated speedups from zero-overlap thresholds, \texttt{claim\_check.py} enforces statistical criteria over $N \ge 15$ pairs with two-sided Mann--Whitney $U$ ($p$), 2,000-resample 95\% bootstrap CIs, and $p_5/p_{95}$ tail ratios:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:g1_rules}
g_{\downarrow} = \frac{m_{\mathrm{llama}} - m_{\mathrm{ASI}}}{m_{\mathrm{llama}}},\quad
g_{\uparrow} = \frac{m_{\mathrm{ASI}} - m_{\mathrm{llama}}}{m_{\mathrm{llama}}},\quad
\mathrm{TailR}_{\downarrow} = \frac{p_{95,\mathrm{ASI}}}{p_{95,\mathrm{llama}}} \le 1.10,\quad
\mathrm{TailR}_{\uparrow} = \frac{p_{5,\mathrm{ASI}}}{p_{5,\mathrm{llama}}} \ge 0.90.
\end{equation}
A metric earns \textbf{\texttt{WIN}} if $N \ge 15$, $g \ge 0.03$, $p < 0.01$, and its tail ratio passes; it earns \textbf{\texttt{STRONG WIN}} if additionally $[\min_{\mathrm{ASI}}, \max_{\mathrm{ASI}}]$ and $[\min_{\mathrm{llama}}, \max_{\mathrm{llama}}]$ do not overlap. Overall tier \textbf{\texttt{BEATS}} requires $\ge 1$ primary \texttt{WIN} (\texttt{t1\_ttft}, \texttt{t1\_dec}, \texttt{t2\_ttft}, \texttt{t2\_dec}, or \texttt{long\_prefill\_tps}), $0$ primary losses, and a \texttt{WIN}/\texttt{STRONG WIN} on \texttt{rss\_mb}. \texttt{claim\_check.py} and \texttt{colab\_asi\_benchmark.ipynb} also record \texttt{/proc/stat} \texttt{steal\_jiffies} telemetry to expose hypervisor preemption on shared VMs.

\section{Honest Limitations and Future Work}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Pre-v2.2 $N=15$ / $N=10$ Captures:} Both \texttt{results\_broadwell\_n15.json} (\texttt{G1 Tier: BEATS}) and Colab $N=10$ (\texttt{4e73276}) predate v2.2 (\texttt{dot2=1} defaults, SMT spin-backoff, and \texttt{/proc/stat} \texttt{steal\_jiffies} telemetry). Full $N=15$ G1 re-runs on Broadwell and Colab Xeon via \texttt{colab\_asi\_benchmark.ipynb} remain the immediate empirical priority.
    \item \textbf{Compile-Only Wide-SIMD and ARM64 Paths:} Only AVX2 + FMA + F16C has been executed on physical silicon; AVX-512, AVX-512-VNNI, non-\texttt{F16C}, and ARM64 NEON branches are compile/audit-verified only and require execution on Sapphire Rapids/Zen~4 and ARM64 hardware.
    \item \textbf{DRAM Ceiling and \texttt{lm\_head} Dominance:} On Broadwell (12.08\,GB/s DRAM-RO), 518.34\,MB/tok limits pure DRAM-bound decode to 23.31\,tok/s, with \texttt{lm\_head} consuming 40.24\% of active bytes ($\sim$36\% of decode latency).
    \item \textbf{Lazy \texttt{mmap} Cold-Fault Tradeoff:} Skipping \texttt{LILA\_ASI\_VERIFY\_CRC=1} keeps \texttt{lila\_asi\_open} near 27\,ms, but without \texttt{-{}-prepopulate}, cold page faults shift into Turn-1 TTFT.
    \item \textbf{Dense Architecture Dispatch:} \texttt{g\_asi\_arch\_hybrid\_qwen35} is complete and G0-verified, whereas \texttt{g\_asi\_arch\_dense\_transformer} currently registers \texttt{NULL} handlers in \texttt{asi\_arch.c}.
\end{enumerate}
